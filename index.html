About Vista Cleaning Operations

A family business, carried forward.

This business was built by my mother, Maria Magdalena Ayala Guardado — a hardworking woman who believed that a clean home was a form of care. She poured her heart into every job, and through this work she provided for our family and built something we could all be proud of.

On December 15, 2024, we lost her to an act of senseless violence. Her absence has left a wound in our family that words cannot fully describe. But her spirit, her work ethic, and the business she created live on.

My name is Eddy. I am Maria’s son, and I am continuing what she started. It hasn’t been easy. In the wake of losing her, I made a promise — to protect my sisters, to guide them, and to carry forward the legacy our mother built with her own hands.

Running this business is how I honor her. Every home we clean, we clean with the same dedication and love she brought to this work every single day. When you invite us into your home, you are not just hiring a cleaning service. You are supporting a family that has been through the unimaginable and has chosen to keep going.

We are grateful for every single client, because your trust in us is what makes it possible to keep our mother’s dream alive.

Thank you for being part of our story.
— Eddy Ayala
